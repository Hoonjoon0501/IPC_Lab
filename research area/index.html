KAIST INTEGRATED POWER CIRCUIT LAB

IPC Lab's Vision

The KAIST Integrated Power Circuit (IPC) Lab develops high-performance integrated power electronics for emerging and future applications. Our vision is to advance integrated power circuits toward higher power density, smaller form factors, and system-level integration.

Discrete Power Electronics

  • High power capability
  • Large volume
  • Discrete components

Integrated Power Circuits
(IPC)

  • High power density
  • Compact form factor
  • System-level integration

Fully Integrated PMICs

  • Lower power capability
  • Small volume
  • Fully integrated

FUTURE POWER DELIVERY

Toward Fully Integrated Power Delivery

Same Power Smaller Form Factor

Power semiconductors and passive components are integrated into a compact package beneath the processor, shortening the power delivery path and reducing the space occupied by separate components. This structure enables higher power density while maintaining the same power delivery capability.

Core Research Areas

Circuit, PDN, magnetics, and digital twins for advanced power electronics and future applications.

Circuit

High-efficiency power conversion circuits and topologies for higher power density.

PDN

Low-impedance power distribution networks for stable, efficient power delivery.

Magnetics

Compact magnetic components for high-frequency, high-density power conversion.

Digital Twin

Physics-based modeling and digital twins for system analysis and design optimization.

AI Datacenter

Power delivery for AI processors and data centers

Electric Vehicles

Power conversion for charging and electric mobility

Humanoids

Compact power systems for dynamic robotic loads

RF Systems

Efficient RF power generation and impedance matching

Wireless Power Transfer

Contactless power for sensors and distributed electronics
