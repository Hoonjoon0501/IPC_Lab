KAIST INTEGRATED POWER CIRCUIT LAB

IPC Lab's Vision

The KAIST Integrated Power Circuit (IPC) Lab develops high-performance integrated power electronics for emerging and future applications. Our vision is to advance integrated power circuits toward higher power density, smaller form factors, and system-level integration.

Discrete Power Electronics

  • High power capability
  • Large volume
  • Discrete components

Integrated Power Circuits
(IPC)

  • High power density
  • Compact form factor
  • System-level integration

Fully Integrated PMICs

  • Lower power capability
  • Small volume
  • Fully integrated

FUTURE POWER DELIVERY

Toward Fully Integrated Power Delivery

Same Power Smaller Form Factor

Power semiconductors and passive components are integrated into a compact package beneath the processor, shortening the power delivery path and reducing the space occupied by separate components. This structure enables higher power density while maintaining the same power delivery capability.
