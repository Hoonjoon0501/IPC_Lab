Application 04

RF Systems

RF power generation and matching

Kilowatts of radio-frequency power create the plasmas used in semiconductor manufacturing, and drive systems from medical imaging to fusion research.

The challenge is to generate that power efficiently and keep it steady as the load changes in microseconds.

Research topics

01

Kilowatt-class RF amplifiers

Challenge

At megahertz frequencies, switching losses grow quickly.

Approach

Switched-mode amplifiers and layouts that stay efficient at kilowatt output.

02

Fast impedance matching

Challenge

Plasma conditions shift, and mismatched power reflects back.

Approach

Matching networks that track the load quickly and accurately.

03

RF magnetics

Challenge

Air-core inductors are bulky and leak magnetic field.

Approach

Magnetic-core components designed for high-frequency, high-power operation.
